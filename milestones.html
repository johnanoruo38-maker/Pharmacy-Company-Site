Milestones & Recognitions

Forty-three years of growth, from one formulation unit to a national footprint.

Milestones

1983

Sun Pharma established with five psychiatry products and a two-person marketing team.
Sun Pharma set up our first manufacturing facility for tablets and capsules at Vapi, Gujarat, India.

1988 - 1991

We launched Monotrate and Angize, our first cardiology products.
We established our first center to create a base for strong product and process development.

1994 - 1995

We launched Initial Public Offering (IPO).
We began production at API manufacturing plant in Panoil Gujarat, India.

1996 - 1997

We expanded our sales network across 24 countries.
We acquired API plant from Knoll Pharma at Ahmednagar, Maharashtra, India
We purchased equity stakes in TDPL and MJ Pharma.
We had our first international acquisition - Caraco Pharmaceutical Laboratories, USA (completed 100% acquisition in 2010).
We established new research facility in Mumbai, India.

1998 - 1999

We began operations at a new formulation unit in Silvassa, Gujarat, India.
We acquired Milmet Labs, Cephalexin and 7ADCA actives manufacturing site in India.

2000 - 2001

We acquired Pradeep Drug Company in India.
We commenced production at our new formulatons unit in Dadra, India.

2004 - 2005

We set up a new formulation unit in Jammu, India.
We commissioned the first joint venture manufacturing unit in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
We acquired Phlox Pharma (India) and niche brands from Women's First Healthcare (USA).
We completed buyout of manufacturing unit in Bryan, Ohio, USA.
We acquired ICN's business in Hungary from Valeant Pharma.

2007 - 2008

We demerged Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company (SPARC) into a separate entity.
We acquired Chattem Chemicals Inc, USA.

2016 - 2017

We acquired 14 brands from Novartis in Japan.
We acquired Ocula Technologies, Sarl to strengthem our branded ophthalmic portfolio.
We acquired Biosintez to enhance our presence in Russian market
We launched our first branded ophthalmic product, BromSite in USA.
We launched specialty product, Odomzo in USA.

2018 - 2019

We launched specialty product, ILUMYA (tildrakizumab-asmn) in the United states for treatment of moderate-to-severe-plaque psoriasis
We acquired Pola Pharma in Japan to strengthen our global dermatology presence
We launched our specialty product, Cequa in the United states for the treatment of dry eyes
We entered Greater China market through partnership with China Medical System Holdings

2020 - 2021

We completed 25 years of making our debut on the Indian Stock Exchanges
We launched ILUMYA for treatment of plaque psoriasis in Japan.
We launched ILUMYA (tildrakizumab injection), a treatment for adults living with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in Canada
We launched a specialty dermatology product, WINLEVI(clascoterone) cream 1% in the U.S for topical treament of Acne Vulgaris
We forayed into the nutrition bar segment in India with the launch of Revital NXT, India's first nutrition bar with the goodness of natural ginseng, 16 vitamin & minerals and triple blend protein.

2022 - 2023

We acquired Uractiv portfolio from Fiterman Pharma in Romania.
We acquired Concert Pharmaceuticals in USA.

2024 - 2025

Sun pharma included in S&P global sustainability notebook.
We acquired Checkpoint Therapeutics in USA
We launched specialty product, LEQSELVI in USA for treatment of severe alopecia areata.
