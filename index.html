Forty-three years in Indian primary care

Sun Pharmaceuticals Industry LTD is a leading global specialty generic company, with global revenue of over US$ 6.5 billion. Supported by 40 manufacturing facilities, we provide high-quality medicines, trusted by healthcare professionals and patients, to more than 100 countries across the globe. with 1,000 Sun Pharma medicines prescribed every minute, we are driven by a commitment to innovation, improved access and patients care.

We sell through chemists, hospital pharmacies, and government tenders. Every batch carries full traceability back to raw material, and every field representative is trained on the product monograph before they carry it into a clinic.

Read more about Sun Pharma: Our Approach or Our Values

Every formulation starts with a batch record, not a brand brief. Our field team carries the product monograph into the clinic, not just a leave-behind leaflet, and our quality team can trace any strip back to the raw material lot it came from.

  • Access. Price every product so a tier-3 pharmacy can stock it, not just a metro hospital chain.
  • Rigor. Every batch is traceable to its raw material lot, with nothing shipped on reputation alone.
  • Partnership. Train every field representative on the monograph before they carry it into a clinic.
  • Integrity. Report adverse events honestly and fast, even when it slows a launch down.
